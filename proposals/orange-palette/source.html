How this playground calculates colors

Hue, lightness and chroma edit orange’s center color. The contrast lock solves lightness for the chosen white-text ratio. If a color is outside sRGB, chroma is reduced while keeping its lightness and hue direction.

The ten-step trial ramp keeps the center at 500. Step size controls the nearby 400/600/700 shades; the rest extends to light and dark tinted endpoints. Hover and selected always come from this displayed ramp.

“Regenerate from orange” keeps each BP6 family’s hue, matches its center luminance to orange, and shares orange’s relative lightness spacing and chroma profile. Neutral grays stay fixed. This is an experimental model inspired by Ryan, not a reproduction of his quadratic recipe or CIECAM02 dark-mode compensation.
